About The Daily Brief

Sharp, independent journalism covering the stories that matter

Our Mission

Logerithm News was founded on a straightforward premise: people deserve access to clear, accurate, well-sourced reporting on the topics that shape their lives and their world. In an era of information overload and eroding trust in media, we believe the answer is more rigor, not less — more transparency about how we work, more care about what we publish, and more respect for readers' intelligence.

We cover technology, health, science, business, and world affairs. We seek out stories that are genuinely important, explain them clearly without condescension, and are honest about what is known, what is uncertain, and what remains disputed.

Editorial Standards

Every article published by Logerithm News is held to the following standards:

  • Accuracy: Claims are verified before publication. We cite primary sources — peer-reviewed research, official data, direct interviews — wherever possible. We do not publish rumors, unverified claims, or content designed to mislead.
  • Independence: Our editorial decisions are made entirely by our editorial team. Advertisers have no influence over what we cover, how we cover it, or the conclusions we reach. No story has ever been written, killed, or modified at an advertiser's request.
  • Transparency: We identify our sources to the extent possible. When we use anonymous sources, we explain why and provide as much context as we can about their knowledge and position. We distinguish clearly between news reporting, analysis, and opinion.
  • Fairness: We represent the views of people and organizations fairly, including those we disagree with. We do not caricature, misrepresent, or quote out of context.
  • Proportionality: We try to cover topics in proportion to their actual importance, not in proportion to their virality or emotional intensity.

Corrections Policy

We make mistakes. When we do, we correct them promptly, transparently, and prominently. Corrections are noted at the bottom of the relevant article with a description of what was changed and when. We do not quietly edit articles without noting the change.

If you believe an article contains an error, reach out via email. We take all corrections requests seriously and respond to every one.

Our Coverage Areas

  • Technology: Artificial intelligence, consumer technology, cybersecurity, the internet economy, and the social implications of technological change.
  • Health: Medical research, public health, healthcare policy, nutrition, and mental health — with particular attention to distinguishing well-supported findings from preliminary or overhyped research.
  • Science: Climate science and climate technology, space exploration, biology, physics, and environmental research.
  • Business: Economics, labor markets, corporate strategy, entrepreneurship, and the forces shaping how people work and earn.
  • World: International affairs, geopolitics, and global trends that affect daily life.
  • Lifestyle: Evidence-based reporting on wellness, personal finance, education, and how people live.

Advertising and Revenue

Logerithm News is supported by advertising revenue, primarily through Google AdSense. We are transparent about this relationship:

  • All advertisements are clearly labeled as "Advertisement."
  • Advertising placement is automated — we do not control which specific ads appear.
  • Our editorial team does not communicate with our advertising partners about content decisions.
  • We do not accept sponsored content, native advertising, or pay-to-publish arrangements.

For more detail, see our Advertising Disclosure in our Privacy Policy.
